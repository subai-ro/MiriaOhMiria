## Protected seams

### Objective versus subjective

Ledger and process traces may store exact objective evidence for Creator
forensics. A character-facing input must be separately produced by a valid
perception or information path.

### Decision versus resolution

Cognition chooses what to attempt. Resolver code owns physical truth, costs,
partial success, failure, and subsequent objective events.

### Process versus story

Fixed-clock systems operate on state and causal inputs. They do not choose a
narrative recipient and do not infer significance.

### Perception versus motivation

Forming a percept only creates evidence that a future mind may consider. A mind
may ignore it or interpret it incorrectly.

### Player versus world

Projects, processes, institutions, and entities do not require player presence.
The player can intervene in an existing situation but is not assigned as its
solution.
